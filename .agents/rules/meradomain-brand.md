## 3. Platform-Specific Copywriting Rules

### LinkedIn (B2B Authority & Engineering)
- **Objective**: Position MeraDomain as the enterprise choice for high-performance web engineering.
- **Structure**:
  1. Executive provocation or counter-intuitive industry fact.
  2. Technical breakdown (Next.js Server Components, edge caching, Core Web Vitals).
  3. Quantifiable ROI (e.g. sub-second load times, 42% lead lift).
  4. Concise takeaway with 3-5 hashtags (`#WebEngineering #Nextjs #B2B #MeraDomain #BuildLaunchGrow`).
- **Destination**: Direct citation of `https://meradomain.in`.

### Instagram (Visual Hooks & Dynamic Carousels)
- **Objective**: Stop the scroll, educate founders, drive saves and profile visits.
- **Structure**:
  1. 2-line hook.
  2. 5-step carousel narrative (Problem ➔ Architecture ➔ Code/Design ➔ Outcome ➔ Action).
  3. Call-to-action: *"Save this post for your next website overhaul 📌 • Link in bio to book an engineering consult."*
  4. 15-20 curated hashtags.

### Facebook (Community & Practical Business Solutions)
- **Objective**: Engage Indian MSMEs, D2C brands, and startup founders.
- **Structure**:
  1. Relatable business dilemma (e.g., checkout abandonment, mobile lag).
  2. Story of how bespoke digital engineering solved it.
  3. Clear invitation to comment or visit `https://meradomain.in`.

### Google Business Profile (Local SEO Authority)
- **Objective**: Dominate Delhi NCR and Indian local search rankings for web design and AI software development.
- **Key Geo-Signals**: Connaught Place, New Delhi, Delhi NCR, India.
- **Primary Keywords**: Web design company, custom software development, Next.js web agency, AI automation.
- **Length**: 100-150 words.
- **Call-to-Action**: Action button set to `LEARN_MORE` or `CALL_NOW` pointing to `https://meradomain.in`.
